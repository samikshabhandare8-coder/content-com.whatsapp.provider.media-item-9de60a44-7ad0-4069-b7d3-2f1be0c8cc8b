The Problem

Every box is used once. Most last less than a week — some, five seconds.

E-commerce runs on packaging that's designed to be thrown away the moment it's opened. Sellers keep paying for it. The planet keeps absorbing it.

100B+Cardboard boxes shipped globally per year
~5 secAverage time a box is handled before it's trash
₹30Cost per single-use box, paid on every single order

Packaging waste is one of the fastest-growing contributors to urban landfill volume — and when tape, bubble wrap, and cardboard mix together, they can't even be recycled cleanly. The real cost of a box isn't ₹30. It's a planet paying for it.

How It Works

Four steps. One loop. No landfill.

Re-Pack runs on the courier routes that already exist — we just make the box worth keeping.

Unit Economics

The math sellers actually care about.

Sellers currently pay ₹30 for a single-use box that's used once. Re-Pack charges ₹18 per trip — but because each container survives 50+ cycles, real cost per use drops to just ₹5 after the 10th cycle.

That's a 70% gross margin for us, and a straight 40% packaging cost cut for every seller on the platform.
